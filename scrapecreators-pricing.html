ScrapeCreators pricing: what each credit pack costs

The two published packs, the point where the larger one becomes cheaper, and a calculator that picks the cheapest purchase for the credits you need.

Short answer

ScrapeCreators pricing is pay as you go with no subscription: the Freelance pack costs $47 for 25,000 credits ($1.88 per 1,000 requests) and the Business pack costs $497 for 500,000 credits ($0.99 per 1,000 requests). Purchased credits have no expiration date, and one credit is one request on most endpoints.

How much does ScrapeCreators cost per 1,000 requests?

It costs $1.88 per 1,000 requests on the Freelance pack and $0.99 per 1,000 on the Business pack, according to the vendor's home page. Those are the only two rates with a published price. A third option, Enterprise, is listed for buyers of 1M+ credits (1,000,000 or more) at custom pricing, so no figure for it appears here.

These are one-time purchases, not monthly plans. The vendor's wording is "No subscriptions" and "Buy credits when you need them." That changes how you should read the price: a monthly plan elsewhere resets every billing cycle, while a pack here is drawn down for as long as it lasts.

The rate you actually pay per request used depends on how much of a pack you consume. A Business pack bought for a job of 275,000 requests still costs $497, so the effective rate is higher than the headline $0.99 until the remaining credits are spent. The calculator above shows that effective rate for your number.

When is the Business pack cheaper than several Freelance packs?

The Business pack is cheaper once you need more than 250,000 credits. Ten Freelance packs give 250,000 credits for $470, which is still under $497. An eleventh pack takes the total to $517 for 275,000 credits, and at that point one Business pack costs less and delivers 500,000.

Do ScrapeCreators credits expire?

No. The vendor states that purchased credits have no expiration date. It adds that if expiration dates are introduced for future purchases, that will be disclosed before purchase, and that credits bought under the current policy will remain non-expiring.

This is why leftover credits matter less here than on a monthly plan. If the cheapest purchase leaves 225,000 credits unused, they stay on the account for a later job instead of lapsing at the end of a billing cycle.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. New accounts start with 100 free credits, and the home page says no credit card is required. The vendor offers up to 1,200 more as bonuses: 100 for saying how you found the service, 100 for starring its GitHub repository and 1,000 for an honest G2 review.

For a broader look at which vendors offer ongoing free credits and which offer a time-limited trial, see web scraping APIs with a free tier.

What a credit buys, and what the price does not cover

  • One credit is one request on most endpoints. The vendor says a few endpoints use more and that the exact cost is shown in its docs. Per-endpoint costs are not on the pricing section and are not included in the calculator, so treat its result as a minimum if you use those endpoints.
  • Cached results are free. The home page states that a result already in the vendor's cache costs 0 credits.
  • No account-level rate or concurrency caps. The vendor recommends staying below 500 concurrent requests for best reliability.
  • Not published: the Enterprise price, a refund policy on the pages checked, and any sales tax. None of these are estimated here.

How this compares with general scraping APIs

ScrapeCreators sells an API for public social media data, so its credits are not interchangeable with credits from a general-purpose page-fetching API. The billing shape is also different: the general APIs tracked on this site sell monthly plans whose credits reset each cycle, while these packs are bought once and kept.
