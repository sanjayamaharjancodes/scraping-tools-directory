Web Scraping APIs With a Free Tier

"Free tier" and "free trial" get used interchangeably in most roundups, but they're different offers: a free tier keeps working indefinitely at no cost (usually with a low request cap); a free trial gives you a larger allowance for a fixed window, then requires payment. If you're prototyping something you're not ready to pay for yet, this distinction decides which vendors actually fit.

Which kind of "free" fits your situation

  • Learning the API, evaluating fit, no deadline pressure: pick an ongoing free tier (Scrapfly, ScrapingDog, Bright Data, or Apify) so you're not forced into a purchase decision by a countdown.
  • Need to test a specific, larger workload before committing budget: a trial with a bigger one-time allowance (ScraperAPI's 5,000 credits over 7 days, or Zyte's $5/30-day credit) may cover a more realistic test run than a small permanent free tier, as long as you can finish evaluating inside the window.
  • Building something you intend to run indefinitely at very low volume, not just a prototype: check whether the ongoing free tier's cap (200 credits/ScrapingDog, 5,000 records-month/Bright Data) actually covers your real traffic — if not, you'll need a paid plan regardless of which vendor you pick.
