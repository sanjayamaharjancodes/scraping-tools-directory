SERP API Pricing Comparison: What Google Searches Cost by Volume

Short answer

In this SERP API pricing comparison of SerpApi, Serper and DataForSEO, 100,000 Google searches a month cost least at DataForSEO's Standard queue: $60. Serper is next at $75 using its $375 prepaid pack, and SerpApi costs $725 on its Searcher plan. Deeper result pages raise every price: DataForSEO bills each extra 10 results as another page, and SerpApi needs another paginated search for each 10 results. For real-time results, DataForSEO's Live mode costs $2.00 per 1,000, or $200 at the same volume.

How much does a SERP API cost per 1,000 searches?

At list prices, a SERP API costs from $0.60 per 1,000 Google searches (DataForSEO Standard queue) to $25 per 1,000 (SerpApi's smallest paid plan, Starter, which includes 1,000 searches for $25 a month). Volume matters most for SerpApi: its Searcher plan works out to $7.25 per 1,000 and Cloud 1M to $3.75 per 1,000.

Which SERP API billing model wastes the least money?

Pay-as-you-go wastes the least, because you pay only for the result pages you request. The three vendors use three different models, and each loses money in a different place.

SerpApi: monthly subscription. You pay for the plan's allowance whether you use it or not, and its pricing page does not describe carrying unused searches into the next month. If you need 31,000 searches, Big Data (30,000 searches for $275) is too small, so you pay $725 for 100,000 and leave 69,000 unused. In its favor, SerpApi says only successful searches count; cached, errored and failed searches do not. It also has a free plan of 250 searches a month.

Serper: prepaid packs that expire. Each pack's credits stay valid for 6 months. The Starter pack is $50 for 50,000 credits ($1.00 per 1,000); larger packs fall to $0.75, $0.50 and finally $0.30 per 1,000 for 12,500,000 credits. At low volume, expiry is the cost: at 1,000 searches a month you use 6,000 credits in six months and 44,000 expire, so the $50 pack works out to $8.33 a month. At higher volume the bigger packs win, which is how 1,000,000 searches a month come to $500 on the Scale pack ($0.50 per 1,000). New accounts get 2,500 free queries.

DataForSEO: pay per result page. There is no monthly allowance to waste. Standard queue tasks cost $0.60 per 1,000, Priority $1.20 and Live mode $2.00. Standard and Priority are queued tasks; Live is the real-time option, so compare Live with SerpApi and Serper if your app waits on each search. The pricing page does not state a minimum deposit or a free-trial amount.

What happens when you need more than 10 results per query?

Costs multiply at both SerpApi and DataForSEO. SerpApi's blog says Google now usually returns 10 organic results per request and that getting more takes pagination, which adds cost; this page counts each 10-result page as one SerpApi search. DataForSEO bills per SERP of up to 10 organic results, adds one base price for each further 10, and rounds depth up: a depth of 15 is billed as 20 results. Serper's pricing page does not say how deeper pages are billed, so it is left out here.

    What these numbers do not cover

    All figures are USD list prices before sales tax, for Google organic results only. They leave out other search engines, other Google result types, enterprise quotes and any extra charges for optional parameters. SerpApi lists Legal Shield from the Production plan ($150/mo) up and ZeroTrace on Cloud plans; this page does not put a price on those features. It also does not compare result quality, speed or success rates, and no tests were run for it.
