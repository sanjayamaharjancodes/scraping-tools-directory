Web Scraping API Pricing Compared by Monthly Cost

Short answer

Among ScrapingBee, Scrapfly, ScrapingDog and ScraperAPI, web scraping API paid plans start at $19 a month, and 100,000 plain requests cost $30 to $49. These are four credit-plan vendors, not the whole market, and this compares list prices, not performance. Plain means no JavaScript rendering, base proxy, unprotected target.

Lowest list price:

  • 1,000,000 plain requests: ScrapingDog, $90/mo with dynamic=false.
  • JavaScript rendering on: ScrapingDog, $90/mo for 100,000 and $350/mo for 1,000,000.

    How do you estimate a mixed workload?

    Add up credits before you compare prices. Take an illustrative month of 300,000 plain requests and 20,000 rendered requests. That is 400,000 credits at ScrapingDog and ScrapingBee, 420,000 at Scrapfly and 500,000 at ScraperAPI. The smallest plans that cover those totals are ScrapingDog Standard at $90, ScrapingBee Startup at $99, Scrapfly Pro at $100 and ScraperAPI Startup at $149.

    Why can't you compare credits directly?

    A credit buys different work at each vendor. A rendered request costs 5 credits at ScrapingBee and ScrapingDog, 6 at Scrapfly and 10 at ScraperAPI, which is why rendering reorders the widget's list.

    A worked example. ScrapingBee's Freelance plan is $49 for 250,000 credits, or $0.196 per 1,000 classic-proxy requests without rendering. With JavaScript rendering at 5 credits, it covers 50,000 requests, or $0.98 per 1,000. A premium proxy with rendering (25 credits) covers 10,000 requests, or $4.90 per 1,000.

    What happens when the credits run out?

    • ScraperAPI pauses scraping by default until credits renew. Opt-in auto-upgrade moves Hobby through Professional to the next plan, and pay-as-you-go on Scaling and above runs up to a monthly cap you set. The balance resets at renewal, so unused credits don't carry over.
    • Scrapfly Discovery hard-caps at quota, while Pro and above switch to pay-as-you-go. Its billing docs cap extra usage at 50% of monthly quota on non-custom plans, then suspend the account until renewal or upgrade. Each project can also set a Budget Limit or turn pay-as-you-go off. The docs and pricing page don't say whether unused credits roll over at a normal renewal.
    • ScrapingDog requests return an over-limit response until credits reset on your next billing date. From Standard upward, unused credits carry over to the next billing cycle.
    • ScrapingBee: its docs list a 401 response for being out of credits, which is not billed. Credits are valid for one month and don't roll over.

    How does geotargeting differ?

    • ScrapingBee includes it from Hobby ($19), but unsupported classic-proxy locations fall back to the US. Its pricing page and that post don't state a credit cost for classic-proxy geotargeting.
    • ScraperAPI Hobby and Startup are US & EU only, with Global on the plan cards from Business ($299); the vendor's comparison table calls it country-level.
    • ScrapingDog lists a request with a country or geo set at 10 credits.
    • Scrapfly's pricing page lists no separate geotargeting price or plan limit, and says credit cost per request depends on the features you enable.

    Vendor summaries

    ScraperAPI

    Plans run from $49/mo (100,000 credits, 20 concurrent threads) to $1,975/mo (21,500,000 credits, 500 threads). Professional and Advanced carry limited-offer bonus-credit labels, and the page does not say whether the listed totals include them. A standard page costs 1 credit, Amazon 5, Google and Bing 25, LinkedIn 30, and bot-protected sites add 10. Its docs say it charges only for 200 and 404 responses and for requests you cancel before its 70-second window ends.

    Scrapfly

    Browser rendering is off by default. Discovery allows 5 concurrent requests. Pro, Startup and Enterprise allow 20, 50 and 100, then bill $3.50, $2.00 and $1.20 per 10,000 extra credits. A residential proxy is 25 credits. Its billing docs also charge bandwidth past the first 1MB of a text response at binary rates, include only 3MB free with rendering, and say the Unblocker can upgrade the proxy pool, which can affect the final cost.

    ScrapingDog

    A premium proxy costs 10 credits, or 25 with rendering. Lite is $40/mo (200,000 credits, 5 concurrency), and the list continues to Nova Pro at $30,000/mo. The vendor states "Failed requests never charged."

    ScrapingBee

    Hobby is $19/mo (75,000 credits, 25 concurrent requests), and the price list reaches $5,799/mo for 120,000,000 credits. Geotargeting covers 200+ countries and territories (40+ on classic proxies).
