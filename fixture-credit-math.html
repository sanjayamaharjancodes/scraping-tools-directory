How Many ScraperAPI Credits Does a Million Requests Use?

A small calculator that turns request volume and credit multipliers into a monthly plan.

Short answer

One million standard requests use 1,000,000 credits, which fits the $149 Startup plan exactly with no headroom. Amazon requests cost 5 credits each, so 100,000 of them use 500,000 credits.

Why the multiplier matters more than the request count

A credit plan sells a pool of credits, not a count of pages. The Hobby plan costs $49 a month and includes 100,000 credits. Whether that is 100,000 pages or far fewer depends entirely on what each request asks the service to do. Plain requests cost one credit. Requests for protected retail pages cost more, and the vendor documents the multiplier for each category on its own credit page.

That is why the same monthly volume can land on very different plans. Budgeting from the headline number alone tends to under-count by a wide margin when rendering or retail targets are involved, so the safest habit is to multiply first and compare plans second.
