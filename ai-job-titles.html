AI job titles: what 3,958 company job boards actually post

First-hand counts from the job index behind ATS Jobs Search, made by Scaleturn, which publishes this site. The index reads employers' own career pages on six applicant tracking systems (ATS). This page counts the job titles in it that name AI or machine learning.

Short answer

The AI job titles companies post most are Machine Learning Engineer (on 556 company job boards, counting "ML Engineer") and AI Engineer (539), then Applied AI Engineer (147), AI/ML Engineer (88), AI Product Manager (59) and AI Platform Engineer (57). Of 456,681 open jobs on 18,770 boards on 19 Nov 2026, 19,600 (4.29%), on 3,958 of those boards, have a title that names AI or machine learning. Almost a quarter of those (23.1%) are AI-training and data gigs from just 41 boards. And Forward Deployed Engineer, a title without the word AI, is on more boards (563) than any title that names it.

    AI Engineer or Machine Learning Engineer?

    Across all six ATSs the two are level: Machine Learning Engineer on 556 boards, AI Engineer on 539. Which one an employer writes depends on where it posts. Machine Learning Engineer leads on Greenhouse (242 boards to 176) and Ashby (206 to 175). AI Engineer leads on Workable (71 to 41), Lever (53 to 44), Recruitee (36 to 13) and Personio (28 to 10). By listings, Machine Learning Engineer leads, 1,161 to 842. Applied AI Engineer comes third with 147 boards, 86 of them on Ashby. A job seeker should search for all three titles, and for AI/ML Engineer.

    The training group holds titles such as "AI Trainer", "AI Training Contributor" and "Freelance AI Trainer Project", often after a profession or a language: work that produces data to train and test AI models, freelance by many titles' own wording. It is 23.1% of AI-named listings (4,521), from only 41 boards. The largest, Prolific Academic Ltd on Greenhouse, posts 2,865 of them (63.4%); with its other AI-named titles it lists 2,874, 14.7% of all, and the five largest boards hold 26.6%. Without the training gigs, 15,079 listings name AI: 3.3% of all open listings. So read an AI title to the end: one that starts with a profession and ends in "AI Training" asks for that knowledge to train a model, and is not a job in that profession.

    Entry-level AI jobs are rare

    Titles that name AI are more senior than the rest. Leaving out the training gigs, 41.0% carry a senior word (Senior, Staff, Principal, Lead, Head, Director and the like), against 23.0% of all listings, and only 4.7% (711 listings) a junior, intern, student or graduate word, close to the 4.4% of all listings. Research has the highest junior share, 16.0%. For someone starting out, AI titles with no seniority word (54.2%) are the next place to look.

    Method and limits

    • Source: the ATS Jobs Search index built at 04:03 UTC on 19 Nov 2026 from employers' public job feeds on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Recruitee, Personio and Workable: 461,985 open jobs on 23,287 boards. A job with the same title, location and posting time on two boards of one ATS counts once, which removes 5,304 copies and leaves 456,681 listings on 18,770 boards. Employers on other systems, such as Workday or iCIMS, are not covered, so the shares describe these boards rather than the whole job market.
    • Names AI: the title contains one of a fixed list of terms, among them AI, ML, LLM, KI (German), NLP, machine learning, generative AI, computer vision and agentic (full list in the JSON); short forms must be in capitals. Two rules leave out 7 titles: "AI" as another language's word in an all-capitals title, and LLM as the Master of Laws degree in a legal title.
    • Titles only: the index has no job descriptions or salaries, so an AI job titled plain "Software Engineer" is not counted. Groups and seniority come from keyword rules, so some titles land in the wrong group; every rule is in the JSON.
    • Boards, not companies: a company can run more than one board, and a board is counted once per title.
    • Reproducible: build_data.py rebuilds the CSV and JSON from the index files, refuses any other index build by its SHA-256 hashes, and fails if a number on this page is not in that data. The index files are not published; the CSV and JSON carry every count used. This page was drafted with AI tools from that data.

    Search these jobs yourself

    ATS Jobs Search searches the same index by title keyword, location and posting date, so a search for "machine learning engineer" or "forward deployed" returns these listings with their links. Developers can read the feeds directly with the open-source ats-job-feed library. Scaleturn makes both; these are not affiliate links. The same index shows what day most jobs are posted and how old open job postings are, and our ATS job posting API crawl data shows how reliable the public feeds are.
