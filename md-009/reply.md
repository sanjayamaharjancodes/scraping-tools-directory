Rather than use the offered credits, we ran Firecrawl's public free plan through our published benchmark method on 22 Nov 2026: 95 requests across 19 public pages, with the pages, settings and pass rule fixed before the first request. Firecrawl passed 80 of 85 requests to static and JavaScript pages (all five misses were one page that loads its content after 10 seconds, at default settings) and 10 of 10 on two Cloudflare-protected practice pages, with a median of about 2.2 seconds per page. On that result it now appears in our comparison as the one tested entry, though at list price it is the most expensive of the five credit-plan APIs there for plain pages ($99 for 100,000 a month): https://scrapetools.scaleturn.com/#firecrawl-test.
