ScraperAPI vs ScrapingBee: Pricing, Credits and Limits

Both are single-endpoint, credit-based scraping APIs. This page compares their published plans, credit costs and trials, and lets you price your own mix of plain and JavaScript-rendered pages.

Short answer

In the ScraperAPI vs ScrapingBee comparison, at $49 a month, ScrapingBee gives more: 250,000 credits and 50 concurrent requests on its Freelance plan, against 100,000 credits and 20 threads on ScraperAPI's Hobby plan.

ScrapingBee also charges less for JavaScript rendering (5 credits a request vs 10), but it renders by default, so a plain page costs 1 credit only if you switch rendering off. ScraperAPI has the larger trial: 5,000 credits for 7 days, vs 1,000 credits at ScrapingBee.

Which is cheaper, ScraperAPI or ScrapingBee?

ScrapingBee is cheaper at most published volumes, but ScraperAPI wins two bands: above 3,000,000 and up to 5,000,000 credits, and above 8,000,000 and up to 10,500,000 credits.

At the low end the gap is wide. Per 1,000 credits, ScraperAPI's Hobby plan works out to $0.49 and ScrapingBee's Freelance plan to $0.196. For 1,000,000 credits, ScrapingBee's Startup plan is $99 and ScraperAPI's Startup plan is $149, a $50 difference. For 3,000,000 credits the plans are $249 and $299, again $50 apart.

Just above 3,000,000 credits, ScrapingBee's next plan is Business + at $599, while ScraperAPI's Scaling plan covers up to 5,000,000 credits for $475, which is $124 less. Just above 8,000,000 credits, ScraperAPI's Professional plan at $975 is $24 below ScrapingBee's Enterprise 14 at $999. If you need more than 75,000 but no more than 100,000 credits, both cost $49.

ScrapingBee also has a smaller entry point: a $19 Hobby plan with 75,000 credits. ScraperAPI's cheapest paid plan is $49. ScraperAPI offers 10% off with yearly billing; ScrapingBee's pricing page does not mention an annual discount.

Credits are not pages, though. Both vendors charge more credits for rendering, premium proxies and hard targets, so the plan that looks cheaper per credit is only cheaper if your traffic mix stays close to plain requests. Price your own mix below.

How much does JavaScript rendering cost on each?

A rendered request costs 10 credits on ScraperAPI and 5 credits on ScrapingBee. A plain request costs 1 credit on both, but ScrapingBee fetches through a headless browser by default, so you have to turn rendering off to get the 1-credit rate.

That makes the $49 plans look very different for rendered pages: ScraperAPI's 100,000 credits cover 10,000 rendered pages, while ScrapingBee's 250,000 credits cover 50,000.

Premium proxies cost more on both. ScraperAPI lists 25 credits for premium proxies plus rendering. ScrapingBee lists 10 or 25 credits depending on rendering, and 75 credits for its stealth proxy. ScraperAPI also prices some domains higher: Amazon 5 credits, Google and Bing 25, LinkedIn 30, plus a 10-credit surcharge per request on sites its pricing page classes as bot-protected. For those, use the ScraperAPI credits calculator.

Which has the better free trial?

ScraperAPI's trial is larger: 5,000 credits for 7 days with no credit card, against 1,000 free credits at ScrapingBee, also with no card. Counted as rendered pages, that is 500 on ScraperAPI and 200 on ScrapingBee. ScraperAPI's FAQ separately mentions a free plan of 1,000 credits with up to 5 concurrent connections. See what the ScraperAPI trial buys and the free tier comparison.

What these numbers do not cover

These are list prices and credit rules from each vendor's own pages. They say nothing about success rates, speed or how often a target needs premium proxies, and neither tool has been benchmarked here (see the methodology page). ScraperAPI says it charges only for successful responses (200 and 404). Unused ScraperAPI credits reset at renewal.
